# About the Santoro Family Foundation

- 🪪 The [Santoro Family Foundation](https://www.santorofamilyfoundation.org) is a private foundation investing in education, economic empowerment, and health.
- 📝 [Our work](https://www.santorofamilyfoundation.org/about) focuses on helping people realize their potential by reducing barriers to learning, earning, and well-being.
- 💥 The Foundation was [founded in 2026](https://www.santorofamilyfoundation.org/stories/introducing-the-santoro-family-foundation) by [Phil Santoro](https://www.santorofamilyfoundation.org/team/phil-santoro) and [Rubi Santoro](https://www.santorofamilyfoundation.org/team/rubi-santoro).
- 📰 Read the latest [stories, ideas, and news](https://www.santorofamilyfoundation.org/stories) from the Foundation.
- 📫 Learn more through our [FAQ](https://www.santorofamilyfoundation.org/faq) or [contact us](https://www.santorofamilyfoundation.org/contact).
